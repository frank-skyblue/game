### V1

- Features a very basic style
- 4 playable classes
  - Rifle, average at everything
  - SMG, fast runner, fast shooting tanky
  - Sniper, slow but deadly
  - Sword, swordsman

### V2

- Upgraded visuals
- 4 playable characters
  - Cpl. Smith
    - Your average rifleman
    - Loadout
      - Rifle
      - Pistol
      - Grenade (can be thrown over walls)
  - Alex One-shot
    - Deadly veteran sniper
    - Loadout
      - Rifle
      - Knife
      - Ability: Stealth
        - Goes into stealth for 10 seconds, gaining faster movement speed
        - Attacking or taking too much damage ends the stealth early
        - Cool down: 10 seconds
  - Shinrai Kagekiya
    - Master swordsman
    - Loadout
      - Sword
      - Shrunken
      - Ability: Dash
        - Dashes forward for a short distance
